VR Lab

Step inside.

These experiences are built for the class VR headsets. Put one on, open its web browser, and go to mrscandrett.github.io/vr.html. Every one also opens as a flat preview on a laptop, so nobody is left out while they wait for a turn.

Checking this device…

Before you put it on

  • Clear a space about two big steps across, or stay seated. Every experience works sitting down.
  • Move by pointing at the floor and pulling the trigger (or pinching). Flick the thumbstick to turn.
  • Keep sessions to about 10–15 minutes. If you feel dizzy or sick, take the headset off and rest.
  • Follow the headset maker’s age guidance and your class rules.

Playing lab PC games on a Quest instead? Follow the Steam & VR Launch Protocol.

Experiences

    Using the rest of the site in a headset

    In a headset browser the whole site switches to VR mode: bigger buttons, a ring around whatever your laser is pointing at, and a 🥽 VR menu in the bottom-left corner with:

    • Full view — makes the simulation, app or video on screen fill the headset window.
    • On-screen keys — arrow keys, Space and Enter for sims and games that expect a keyboard.
    • A− / A+ — text size.

    Change it any time under Settings → Access → VR mode (Auto, On or Off).

    WebXR runs in the browser: no installs, no accounts. Nothing you do in these experiences leaves the headset.
