⚡ AI Tools: Use Responsibly

Before accessing AI assistants, understand the environmental impact of AI and commit to responsible usage.

🌍 AI Environmental Facts You Should Know:

Energy Consumption: Training a single large AI model can consume as much electricity as 100 U.S. homes use in a year.
Carbon Footprint: Every AI query you run uses real energy. A single ChatGPT conversation can use as much electricity as charging your phone.
Water Usage: AI data centers use massive amounts of water for cooling—some require millions of gallons daily.
Resource Extraction: The servers, GPUs, and chips that power AI require mining rare earth elements, which damages ecosystems.
Your Responsibility: Every unnecessary query adds to this impact. Use AI intentionally—not for procrastination or time-wasting.

✓ I Pledge To Use AI Responsibly:

This is serious. AI technology has real environmental consequences. Your actions matter. By using these tools thoughtfully, you're making a choice to minimize waste and respect the planet. Now that you've read this, make sure you actually understand and believe these pledges before clicking access.

Use AI Well

AI belongs in the workflow, but not in place of thinking. Use it to accelerate ideas, debugging, and explanation, not to skip the learning.

Use AI for
  • brainstorming ideas
  • explaining a concept another way
  • debugging and improving your own work
Do not use AI for
  • copying full projects you do not understand
  • skipping the design, math, or writing process
  • turning in work you cannot explain
