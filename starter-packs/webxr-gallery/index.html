WEBXR GALLERY STARTER

The Long Room

A gallery of your projects, laid out as a room. This pack builds the part that has to work first: a plan of the room anybody can read, and an exhibit list that carries every word of the content on its own.

There is no 3D and no headset code in this pack, on purpose. Read README-FIRST.md before you add either — it explains what real WebXR needs, and why a room nobody can enter is still worth building.

Exhibits

This is the gallery. Not a description of the gallery — the gallery. If you later add a 3D room, it shows these same exhibits in another form, and this list stays the version that always works.

    The walk

    A gallery has an order. Somebody reading the list, somebody clicking through the plan, and somebody walking a 3D room should all meet the exhibits in the same sequence, or three visitors get three different exhibitions.

    Before you add 3D

    1. Replace all four exhibits with your own work, including the medium and the summary. Placeholder text under a 3D room is worse than no room.
    2. Unplug the mouse. Reach every exhibit, in order, with Tab and Enter alone.
    3. Zoom the page to 200% and read it again. Then narrow the window to 375px wide and check nothing scrolls sideways.
    4. Only then read the WebXR section of README-FIRST.md, and add the 3D view as an extra way to see what is already here.
