Web Portfolio Starter Pack
Original teaching material by Mr. Scandrett's ClassroomOS.

WHAT IS IN HERE
All HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the mark in images/monogram.svg were written and
drawn for this pack. No third-party art, photographs, fonts, audio, code
libraries, or frameworks are bundled, and nothing is downloaded from the internet
when the page runs. The card thumbnails are CSS patterns with a visible label, not
photographs, so nothing here can be mistaken for real work that does not exist.

The sample projects, the name in the header, and the case study are all clearly
marked placeholders. They do not describe a real person. Replace every one of them
with your own work before you publish.

WHEN YOU ADD YOUR OWN MEDIA
Record every file you did not make yourself: what it is, who made it, the exact
URL you took it from, and the licence that allows you to use it. "Found on Google
Images" is not a licence. If you cannot name the licence, do not ship the file.

Screenshots of your own projects are your own work and need no permission. A
screenshot of somebody else's game, site, or artwork is theirs, and belongs in the
