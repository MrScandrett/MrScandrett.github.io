Web Business Starter Pack
Original teaching material by Mr. Scandrett's ClassroomOS.

THE BUSINESS IS FICTIONAL
"Kestrel Lane Cycle Works", its address (12 Kestrel Lane, Riverbend), its phone
number ((555) 010-0142), its email address (hello@kestrel-lane.example), its
prices and its opening hours are all invented for teaching. The email domain ends
in .example, which is reserved so that nobody can ever own it. No real business,
person, or logo is represented here. If you invent your own business for this
pack, keep it obviously invented, and never publish a page that could be mistaken
for a real shop's own site.

WHAT IS IN HERE
All HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the mark in images/logo.svg were written and drawn
for this pack. No third-party art, photographs, fonts, audio, code libraries, or
frameworks are bundled, and nothing is downloaded from the internet when the page
runs.

LICENSE FOR THE STARTER CODE
CC BY 4.0. Keep it, change it, and publish it, as long as "Mr. Scandrett's
ClassroomOS" is credited somewhere sensible - a footer line or a note in this file
is plenty.

WHEN YOU ADD YOUR OWN MEDIA
Record every file you did not make yourself: what it is, who made it, the exact
URL you took it from, and the licence that allows you to use it. "Found on Google
Images" is not a licence. If you cannot name the licence, do not ship the file.
Photographs of real shops, real staff, or real customers need the permission of
the people in them - that is not only a licence question.

Example of the line to write:
  images/workshop.jpg - photo by A. Example, https://example.com/photo-page,
  Creative Commons CC BY 4.0, cropped by me.
