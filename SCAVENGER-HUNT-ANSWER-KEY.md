## 📝 NOTES FOR PROCTORS

1. **Question #29 (Video Library count):** You'll need to visit the Video Library page yourself to confirm the exact number before grading. Update this answer key if the count has changed.

2. **Common wrong answers to watch for:**
   - Q1: Students might say 5 (confusing with STEAM acronym) — correct is 7
   - Q3: Students might only count NASA books (14) and forget recipes (15) — accept "29 total"
   - Q9: Large numbers are tricky — watch for "43 million" or "43 billion"
   - Q13: Students might only name one (oscilloscope OR TV) — they should name both
   - Q27: "Atom" something or "Molecular simulator" are close — accept if it's recognizably similar

3. **Integrity checks:**
   - Watch for teams copying answers from each other
   - Ensure students are actually visiting the website, not just guessing
   - For photo evidence, you can ask them to screenshot a specific lesson as proof they visited

4. **Tie-breaker (if needed):**
   - First team to submit all correct answers wins
   - If tied on time, ask them to name ONE interactive feature they discovered
