Mathematics · Lesson 09

Linear Equations & Slope

Slope as rise over run Slope-intercept form Reading a line from two points

Every straight line has a steepness and a starting height. In this lesson, students drag two points on a coordinate plane to see slope and the y-intercept update live, then practice writing and reading equations in y = mx + b form.

Learning Target

I can find the slope and equation of a line from two points and explain what each part means.

By the end of the lesson, students should be able to compute slope as rise / run, identify the y-intercept from a graph or table, and write the resulting equation in slope-intercept form.

Slope-Intercept Form

A line can always be written as y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept — the value of y when x is 0.

Slope (m)How steep the line is. Slope = rise / run = (change in y) / (change in x) between any two points on the line.
Y-intercept (b)Where the line crosses the y-axis. It is the starting value before x changes anything.

Worked Example

Find the equation of the line through (1, 2) and (4, 8).

m = (8 - 2) / (4 - 1)
Slope is change in y over change in x between the two points.
m = 6 / 3 = 2
The line rises 2 units for every 1 unit it runs right.
2 = 2(1) + b
Substitute one point and the slope into y = mx + b to solve for b.
b = 0
Solve: 2 = 2 + b, so b = 0.
y = 2x
Final equation in slope-intercept form.

Exit Ticket

Students answer these in complete sentences or with shown work.

  • What does it mean for a line to have a negative slope? Sketch an example.
  • Write the equation of a line that passes through the origin with slope 5.
  • Explain the difference between the slope and the y-intercept in your own words.
Slope-Intercept Form
y = mx + b m is the slope, b is the y-intercept.
Slope rise over run between any two points on the line.
Common Trap
Negative slope The line falls left to right; run stays positive, rise becomes negative.
Lesson Flow
Warm-up Ask students to describe what "steep" means for a hill vs. a line.
Mini lesson Model computing slope from two labeled points.
Practice Have students drag the points to hit target slopes you call out.
Check Use the exit ticket to confirm students can separate slope from intercept.
