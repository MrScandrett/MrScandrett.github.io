Marc Andreessen

Co-creator of NCSA Mosaic (1993) Co-founder of Netscape Communications (1994) The <img> Tag & Visual Web Commercial Internet Pioneer

Marc Andreessen (born 1971) is the American software engineer and entrepreneur who co-wrote NCSA Mosaic in 1993 and co-founded Netscape Communications in 1994. While Tim Berners-Lee created the hypertext architecture of the web, Andreessen made the web visual by introducing the <img> tag and building the first easy-to-use graphical browser for Windows, Mac, and Unix. Netscape turned the internet from an academic curiosity into the mass medium that reshaped global commerce, culture, and daily life.

Who Is Marc Andreessen?

Marc Lowell Andreessen was born on July 9, 1971, in Cedar Falls, Iowa, and grew up in New Lisbon, Wisconsin. Intrigued by computers from childhood, he taught himself BASIC on a TRS-80 before studying computer science at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), graduating in December 1993.

While working as a \$6.85-an-hour student programmer at UIUC's National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA) in late 1992, Andreessen discovered Tim Berners-Lee's World Wide Web project. At the time, browsing the web meant either having an expensive, specialized NeXT workstation (which few people owned) or using a text-only "Line Mode" terminal browser that displayed plain monochrome text without pictures or clickable graphics.

Andreessen teamed up with seasoned NCSA staff programmer Eric Bina. Working night shifts fueled by coffee and sugar, they created NCSA Mosaic. Unlike existing tools, Mosaic placed full-color images directly inside paragraphs of text using a brand-new HTML tag they invented: <img src="...">. They also gave the browser back/forward buttons, bookmarks, and a clean graphical interface. Other students at NCSA ported Mosaic to Apple Macintosh and Microsoft Windows. Within months, tens of thousands of people around the world downloaded Mosaic, sparking the visual web explosion.

In early 1994, former Silicon Graphics chairman Jim Clark met Andreessen. Together, they founded Mosaic Communications Corporation (soon renamed Netscape Communications Corporation) in Mountain View, California. Netscape hired the core Mosaic engineering team and built Netscape Navigator from the ground up. Navigator added SSL encryption for secure credit cards (launching modern e-commerce), cookies for persistent sessions, and hired Brendan Eich to invent JavaScript. When Netscape held its Initial Public Offering (IPO) on August 9, 1995, it ignited the dot-com boom and signaled that the internet had become a cornerstone of the global economy.

  1. 1971 Born on July 9 in Cedar Falls, Iowa.
  2. 1992 Works as an undergraduate programmer at the National Center for Supercomputing Applications.
  3. Jan 1993 With Eric Bina, releases NCSA Mosaic 1.0 for Unix, introducing inline images and point-and-click navigation.
  4. April 1994 Co-founds Netscape Communications with Jim Clark, assembling top Mosaic developers.
  5. Dec 1994 Ships Netscape Navigator 1.0, capturing over 75% of the browser market within months.
  6. Aug 1995 Netscape goes public on Wall Street, kicking off the dot-com era of internet entrepreneurship.
  7. 1995 Netscape introduces SSL (Secure Sockets Layer), allowing encrypted shopping, banking, and confidential web data.
  8. 2011 Publishes his influential thesis explaining how internet-scale software transforms legacy industries.

The Breakthrough: The <img> Tag

When Tim Berners-Lee designed HTML in 1990, he envisioned the web as a network of scientific research papers. Early web architects debated images intensely. Traditionalists argued that images should never be mixed with text on the same page because graphics consumed too much bandwidth and interfered with pure typography. They wanted image links to open in a separate window or trigger an external photo viewer application.

📄
The 1992 Text Web

Documents were monochrome text. Links to figures were marked [IMAGE: chart.gif]; readers had to wait and open another desktop program.

🖼️
The <img> Invention

Andreessen and Bina cut through academic debate by simply adding <img src="..."> to Mosaic, embedding graphics directly inside paragraphs.

🌍
Mass Adoption

Museums, newspapers, bands, schools, and families suddenly saw the web as a visual canvas, triggering the explosion of public web publishing.

<img src="https://example.com/portrait.jpg" alt="A portrait of the inventor" />
<img> The self-closing inline image element introduced in Mosaic
src Source URL: where the browser fetches the image bytes
alt Accessible description for screen readers and slow connections

Andreessen announced the <img> tag on the www-talk mailing list on February 25, 1993, changing the web forever.

Andreessen's Key Contributions

🌐
NCSA Mosaic (1993)

Co-authored the first popular graphical browser for Unix, Mac, and Windows with intuitive point-and-click hyperlinking.

🖼️
The <img> Tag

Introduced inline images to HTML, transforming the web from monochrome scientific papers into a vibrant visual publishing canvas.

🧭
Netscape Navigator (1994)

Co-founded Netscape and created Navigator, which captured over 80% market share and launched the dot-com internet era.

🔒
SSL & Web Security (HTTPS)

Spearheaded Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption at Netscape, enabling secure credit card transactions and the birth of e-commerce.

🍪
HTTP Cookies & State

Introduced cookies to browsers, allowing websites to remember shopping carts, login states, and user preferences across visits.

🚀
Backing the Interactive Web

Hired Brendan Eich to create JavaScript at Netscape, providing the third pillar of modern front-end web design.

Andreessen's Legacy Today

Every time you look at a photograph in a blog post, add a pair of shoes to an encrypted shopping cart, or tap a bookmark in your browser, you are using interface patterns and network protocols pioneered by Marc Andreessen and his teams at NCSA and Netscape.

By betting that regular people wanted a graphical, intuitive, multimedia internet, Andreessen helped turn Tim Berners-Lee's research tool into the foundational medium of the modern world. His venture firm Andreessen Horowitz continues to back the engineers building AI, decentralized networks, and the next frontier of computing.

Hint: The home of the National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA).

Easy2. What HTML tag did Andreessen and Eric Bina introduce to display graphics directly inside web pages?

Hint: Short for "image", used with the src attribute!

Medium3. Who was the experienced NCSA staff programmer who partnered with Andreessen to write the initial Mosaic code?

Hint: He worked late nights at NCSA and later joined Netscape.

Medium4. Which famous company did Marc Andreessen and Jim Clark co-found in April 1994?

Hint: The creator of Navigator, whose logo was a ship's steering wheel.

Medium5. What vital security protocol did Netscape introduce to enable encrypted e-commerce and online banking?

Hint: The cryptographic foundation that evolved into modern TLS / HTTPS.

Challenge6. What browser feature did Netscape engineer Lou Montulli invent to allow websites to remember user state and shopping carts?

Hint: Named after "magic cookies" in Unix computing.

Medium7. Which programmer did Netscape recruit in 1995 to invent JavaScript for Netscape Navigator?

Hint: The pioneer who created JavaScript in 10 days!

Easy8. In 2011, what phrase did Andreessen coin in the Wall Street Journal to describe the impact of internet software?
