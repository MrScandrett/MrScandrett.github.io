MUSIC THEORY

Rhythm Values

Start with four steady beats. Then change the meter and listen for how the beats group together.

Rhythm pyramid

Pick a row. Each row fills one bar of 4/4. A quarter note gets one beat.

Pizza cookie

Read the time signature

The top number tells how many note units fill a bar. The bottom number names the unit. Each box starts on an accent. Tap the numbered counts inside it.

Why do 3/4 and 6/8 feel different?

Both bars contain six eighth notes. In 3/4, clap them in pairs: ONE-and TWO-and THREE-and. In 6/8, clap them in threes: ONE-la-li TWO-la-li. Tap your foot on each capitalized number.

Feel the dot

A dot adds half the note’s own length. Tap your foot on each number and sing “daa” until the next attack.

Inside the groove

These grooves use 4/4. Each column is one equal slice of time. Read across to follow one instrument. Read down to see which sounds land together.

Rhythm clap-along

Listen for one bar, then echo it for one bar. Clap with the class or tap the pad once per clap to score your timing. The low click keeps the beat.
