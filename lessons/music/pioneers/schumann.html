Life and context

Robert Schumann (1810–1856).

Robert Schumann was a German composer and music critic. Literature shaped his imagination, and his piano collections often suggest characters and scenes. His wife Clara Schumann was a major pianist and composer with an artistic career of her own.

Character pieces and musical storytelling

A character piece can suggest an attitude or scene in a small space. Schumann used contrasting rhythms, textures, and dynamics to make short pieces feel like distinct personalities.

Guided listening

Choose a performance of one of these works. Listen once for its overall character, then again for the detail in the prompt.

  • Kinderszenen, Op. 15: compare the gentle Träumerei with the lively Hasche-Mann.
  • Carnaval, Op. 9: listen for contrasting musical portraits.
  • Piano Concerto in A minor: hear the exchange between soloist and orchestra.

Practice and reflection

  1. Play both sound examples. Describe one feature that changes and one that stays the same.
  2. Which musical evidence supports your character description: tempo, articulation, register, or dynamics?
  3. Create a four-note idea at the piano. Make two versions using today’s musical idea, then explain your choices to a partner.
Check your listening

Compare the same notes with smooth, long durations and short, separated durations. Give each version a character name. A useful answer names a feature you can hear and explains its effect. More than one interpretation is possible.
