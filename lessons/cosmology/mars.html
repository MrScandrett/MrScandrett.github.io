Mars

Foundational STEAM lesson for Mars.

Overview

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and a desert world with a thin carbon dioxide atmosphere. Its characteristic reddish tint comes from oxidized iron minerals (rust) in its regolith, harboring geological giants like Olympus Mons—the solar system's largest volcano—and the grand canyon system of Valles Marineris.

Autonomous Entry, Descent, and Touchdown

Landing a one-ton robotic rover on Mars requires decelerating from 12,000 miles per hour to a gentle stop in under seven minutes. Because radio transmissions take over ten minutes to reach Earth, no human can pilot the craft live. The rover relies entirely on autonomous flight software, terrain-relative navigation cameras, supersonic parachutes, and a rocket-powered sky crane.
