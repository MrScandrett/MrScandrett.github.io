Astronomy & Space Science · Grades 6–12

Exoplanets: Worlds Beyond Our Sun

For centuries humans wondered whether other stars held planets of their own. Since the first confirmed discoveries in the 1990s, astronomers have uncovered thousands of extrasolar worlds—from scorching gas giants skimming star surfaces to rocky planets clustered around dim red dwarfs.

5,800+ Confirmed exoplanets cataloged in NASA’s Exoplanet Archive
4,300+ Known planetary systems across our neighborhood of the Milky Way
1992 / 1995 First confirmed pulsar planets (1992) and Sun-like star planet 51 Pegasi b (1995)

Investigation 1 · Transit Photometry

Catch a Planet by Its Shadow

Most exoplanets are far too faint and close to their star to photograph directly. Instead, telescopes like Kepler and TESS measure a star’s brightness over time. When a planet crosses in front of its star, it blocks a tiny fraction of starlight proportional to its cross-sectional area: ΔF / F ≈ (Rp / R★)².

Field Guide · Real Worlds

Remarkable Confirmed Exoplanets

Unlike our tidy inner-rocky / outer-giant layout, exoplanet surveys revealed super-Earths, lava worlds, and seven-planet resonant chains packed inside Mercury’s orbit.

1995 Nobel Breakthrough

51 Pegasi b (Dimidium)

First planet found around a Sun-like star. A Jupiter-mass giant orbiting in just 4.2 days—proving giant planets can migrate inward.

First Earth-Size in HZ

Kepler-186f

About 1.17 Earth radii orbiting near the outer edge of a cooler K/M dwarf’s habitable zone with a 130-day year.

7 Temperate Worlds

TRAPPIST-1 System

Seven Earth-sized rocky planets locked in orbital resonance around an ultra-cool dwarf star 40 light-years away—all fitting inside Mercury’s orbit.

Ultra-Hot Lava Giant

WASP-12b

So close to its star (1.09-day orbit) that tidal gravity stretches it into an egg shape while intense heat strips away its atmosphere.

Nearest Neighbor

Proxima Centauri b

Just 4.24 light-years away around the Sun’s nearest stellar neighbor, receiving roughly 65% of Earth’s sunlight every 11.2 days.

Directly Imaged

Beta Pictoris b

A young super-Jupiter 63 light-years away whose 8-hour spin rate was measured directly from telescope spectra.

Astrochemistry · Habitability

Reading Alien Air with Starlight

Transmission Spectroscopy

During a transit, a sliver of starlight passes through the thin ring of atmosphere surrounding the planet. Gases like water vapor (H2O), carbon dioxide (CO2), methane (CH4), and sodium absorb specific infrared or optical wavelengths—making the planet appear slightly larger at those colors.

Habitable Zone ≠ Guaranteed Life

The habitable zone only marks where stellar energy allows liquid water on a rocky surface—if the planet has a suitable atmosphere and magnetic shielding. Venus sits near the inner edge yet boils at 465 °C from a runaway greenhouse, while tidal flares from red dwarfs can strip air off close-in worlds.
