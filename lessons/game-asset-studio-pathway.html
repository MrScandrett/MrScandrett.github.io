BRIEF

Studio challenge

Create one believable corner of a game.

Pick a clear art direction: cozy spaceship cabin, abandoned research lab, stylized bedroom, fantasy workshop, or another teacher-approved idea. Reuse the same palette, proportions, and level of detail across every lesson.

DESIGN RULE

One visual language

Decide whether forms are rounded or sharp, surfaces are clean or worn, and proportions are realistic or exaggerated.

TECH RULE

One dependable pipeline

Save source files separately from exported files. Test the exported GLB, not only the Blender scene.

Folder + source rule: Use asset-studio/source for .blend files, asset-studio/textures for images, asset-studio/exports for .glb files, and asset-studio/reference for source records. Use your own work or verified CC0/public-domain textures, HDRIs, skyboxes, and models—never generated or ambiguously licensed assets.
