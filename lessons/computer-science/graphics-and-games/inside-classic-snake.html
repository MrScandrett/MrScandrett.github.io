Remix challenges

A bigger board

Change boardSize from 20 to 30 in script.js. How many more squares fit? The snake array still starts with three segments and grows as you eat.

Different foods

Add a food type beside its coordinates. Draw berries and golden apples in different colors. Give apples three points by tracking score separately from snake length.

Unlockable snakes

Start with a new color in --snake. Unlock a striped skin at five points, then save the unlock in localStorage so it survives a refresh.

A 3D snake

Keep the movement rules in tick(). Replace the drawing in draw() with a 3D scene: put one cube at each segment’s grid position on the floor and angle the camera above it.

Online light cycles

First build two bikes on one keyboard. Keep every tail square to leave a permanent trail. For online play, send turns to a shared WebSocket server that moves both bikes on one clock, checks simultaneous crashes, and broadcasts the board.
