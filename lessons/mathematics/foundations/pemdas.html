PEMDAS & Order of Operations

Parentheses Exponents Multiply or divide left to right Add or subtract left to right

PEMDAS is the shared agreement mathematicians use so one expression has one answer. In this lesson, students collapse expressions step by step in an interactive funnel, test how parentheses reshape an expression's value, and master the left-to-right tie rules.

Target PEMDAS Rules Lab 1 · Expression Funnel Lab 2 · Parentheses Power Worked Example Interactive Check Guided Practice Exit Ticket

Learning Target

I can evaluate numerical expressions using the order of operations.

By the end of the lesson, students should be able to explain why 8 - 2 + 5 is solved left to right, why multiplication does not always happen before division, and why parentheses can change the entire value of an expression.

The PEMDAS Order

PEMDAS names the four priority tiers, not six separate ranks. Multiplication and division share a tier and are solved left to right. Addition and subtraction also share a tier and are solved left to right.

PParenthesesDo grouping symbols first: ( ), [ ], or fraction bars.
EExponentsEvaluate powers such as 32 or 24.
MMultiplySame level as division; move left to right.
DDivideSame level as multiplication; move left to right.
AAddSame level as subtraction; move left to right.
SSubtractSame level as addition; move left to right.
Important rule 1 · M & D TieMultiplication and division are a tie. Solve whichever appears first from left to right.
Important rule 2 · A & S TieAddition and subtraction are a tie. Solve whichever appears first from left to right.

Worked Example

Evaluate 6 + 3 × (8 - 4)2 / 2 one operation at a time.

6 + 3 × (8 - 4)2 / 2
Start with the original expression.
6 + 3 × 42 / 2
P · Parentheses first: 8 - 4 = 4.
6 + 3 × 16 / 2
E · Exponents next: 42 = 16.
6 + 48 / 2
MD (left to right) · Multiply first because × is left of /: 3 × 16 = 48.
6 + 24
MD (left to right) · Divide next: 48 / 2 = 24.
30
AS · Add last: 6 + 24 = 30.

PEMDAS Tiers
P Parentheses or grouping symbols first.
E Exponents and powers second.
MD Multiply or divide from left to right (tied priority).
AS Add or subtract from left to right (tied priority).
Common Traps
18 / 3 × 2 = 12 Division comes first because it appears to the left of multiplication.
8 - 2 + 5 = 11 Subtraction comes first because it appears to the left of addition.
Lesson Flow
Warm-up Ask students to solve 8 - 2 + 5 and compare answers (11 vs. 1).
Lab 1 Funnel Have students predict which operator chip goes first before clicking.
