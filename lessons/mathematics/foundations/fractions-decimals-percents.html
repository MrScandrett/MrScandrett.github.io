Mathematics · Rational Numbers & Equivalence

One Number, Three Costumes

Fractions Decimals Percents Benchmark equivalences Number line models

Imagine an actor changing costumes between scenes: a tuxedo, a chef coat, or a diving suit. The actor is the exact same person under every outfit. A rational number does the same trick: 3/4, 0.75, and 75% are three different outfits for the exact same amount of reality.

Learning Targets

  • Locate fractions, decimals, and percents on a single continuous number line.
  • Convert fluently between fraction slices, base-10 decimal expansions, and out-of-100 percentages.
  • Visualize why multiplying numerator and denominator by the same factor yields equivalent fractions.
  • Apply percentages and fractions to everyday calculations like store discounts, tips, and recipe scaling.

Prerequisites: Division: Think in Groups and Fraction Sense Lab. Next: Proportional Reasoning.

Lab 3 · Why Equivalent Fractions Work

If you have 1/2 of a cake and slice every piece in two, you now have 2 pieces out of 4. You have twice as many slices, but each slice is half as large! The quantity of cake hasn't changed. Multiplying both top and bottom by k is really multiplying by k/k = 1.
