Play smart.
Leave access open.

The lab’s games are shared tools. Use the account assigned to your computer, protect other students’ access, and help the next person get farther than they could alone.

02 /

Run the four-step launch check

Thirty seconds of coordination can prevent a lost save, a locked game, or a classmate being bumped out.

CHECK

Ask who is playing

Check whether another student is using—or waiting for—the same game or shared zSpace account.

CHOOSE

Open an allowed title

Use installed library games. Do not buy, download, uninstall, mod, or change account settings without permission.

PLAY

Protect the setup

Keep food and drinks away. Use clean hands, wrist straps for VR, and classroom-appropriate names and chat.

HAND OFF

Save, close, communicate

Exit the game normally, tell the next student what changed, and follow the online/offline decision below.

03 /

Make the right connection decision

Offline Mode is a courtesy tool for ordinary PC play. VR streaming needs the network path to remain active.

Ordinary PC game

Finish online → leave Steam offline

Use this after a non-VR game when another student may need to use the same title or shared access.

  1. Save, then close the game completely.
  2. In Steam, open Steam → Go Offline…
  3. Choose Enter Offline Mode and let Steam restart.
  4. Leave the assigned account signed in.
Steam Link / PC VR

Stay connected while playing

Steam Link sends the VR picture and sound from the PC to the headset and sends controller movement back. Disconnecting breaks that stream.

  1. Keep the PC, Steam, SteamVR, router, and Quest connected.
  2. Do not enter Offline Mode during a VR session.
  3. When finished, exit the VR game and SteamVR normally.
  4. Then free the station for the next student.
Offline Mode has limits: A game normally needs to be installed, fully updated, and launched online once. Games with required online services or outside launchers may not work offline. If Steam says it cannot start offline, reconnect only with teacher permission.

Connect Meta Quest with Steam Link

Prepare the physical play space before the software. A clear boundary and a spotter matter more than launching quickly.

The streaming path

The gaming PC does the heavy rendering. A wired PC connection and a strong 5 GHz Wi-Fi connection to the Quest reduce lag and keep controls responsive.

Prepare the PC. Confirm the assigned account, connect online, and open Steam. Launch SteamVR if it is not already running.

Open Steam Link. On Quest, launch the Steam Link app. The PC and headset should be on the same lab network.

Pair the computer. Select the correct PC. If a PIN appears, enter it on the PC only when Mr. Scandrett approves.

Start the stream. Choose Start Playing, wait for SteamVR Home, then launch an approved title from the library.

End cleanly. Exit the title, close SteamVR, return the headset and controllers, and report low batteries or tracking trouble.

VR safety: Stop immediately if you feel dizzy, nauseated, overheated, or unsteady. Remove the headset slowly, sit down, and tell an adult. Never run, jump, or walk outside the boundary.

07 /

Quick troubleshooting

Use the smallest safe fix. Account, purchase, network-security, and installation problems belong to the teacher.

Steam Link cannot find the PC
Confirm the PC is awake, Steam is open and online, SteamVR is installed, and the Quest is on the same lab Wi-Fi. Do not change router or firewall settings. If it still does not appear, ask for help.
The VR image lags, blurs, or freezes
Pause the session. Move within the approved play area, make sure the PC’s Ethernet cable is connected, close the VR game normally, and retry once. Do not unplug networking equipment.
A game will not start in Offline Mode
That title may need an update, a first online launch, an external account, or a continuous connection. Do not sign out or enter credentials. Ask before taking Steam back online.
Someone else needs the same game
Save and exit normally. For a non-VR session, enter Steam’s Offline Mode when the title supports it. Tell the other student the station is ready. If the game still reports that its license is in use, wait and ask Mr. Scandrett—do not force-close another session.

The real achievement is a clean handoff.

Correct account. Approved title. Safe play space. Saved progress. Appropriate connection mode. Equipment returned. Next student helped.
