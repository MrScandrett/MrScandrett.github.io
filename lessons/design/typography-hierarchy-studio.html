The Architecture of Reading

3

Before someone reads a single sentence, their brain has already mapped the page. Good typography gives readers effortless mental milestones.

4

Typography is not merely choosing pleasing typefaces; it is the discipline of creating spatial relationships. By establishing a clear hierarchy through proportion, weight, and rhythmic leading, designers direct attention with surgical precision, reducing cognitive load and inviting deep immersion.

3. The Mathematics of Modular Scales

Just like musical scales are built on mathematical frequency ratios (octaves $2:1$, fifths $3:2$), graphic designers build typographic scales by multiplying a base size (usually $16\text{px}$) by a chosen ratio:

H1 (Display)31.25 px
H2 (Subhead)25.00 px
H3 (Section)20.00 px
Body (Base)16.00 px
Caption / Footnote12.80 px
