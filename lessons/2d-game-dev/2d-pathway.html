TEACH

Suggested pacing

Play, explain, change one thing.

5–10 min · Play

Open the lab, play the current build, name what changed since last lesson.

10–15 min · Theory

Read the history card and the short code together. Predict before touching sliders.

25–35 min · Build

Students draw, tune or paint in the lab. Everything saves to the shared art store.

10 min · Evidence

Definition-of-done checks need a demonstration or explanation, not just ticks.
