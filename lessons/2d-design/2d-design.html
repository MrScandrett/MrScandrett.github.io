2D Design & Animation

Make a mark. Layer and compose it on a flat surface. Rig it to move. Then make it play.

Everything in this module happens on a two-dimensional picture plane: a painted canvas, a carved woodblock, a stack of raster pixel layers, a rigged stick-figure skeleton, or a scrolling 2D game screen. Study the artists who mastered the flat surface, practice non-destructive photo editing in Layer Lab, animate characters in our in-house Pivot Stick Figure Studio, and ship a complete 2D platformer.

Interactive Warm-Up · How Flat Screens Fake Depth

Five Depth Cues on a 2D Picture Plane

A screen or sheet of paper has only X (width) and Y (height)—there is no physical Z depth. Toggle the five classic 2D depth cues below and drag the camera slider to see how Hokusai's woodblocks, Disney's multiplane camera, and 2D platformer games trick the eye into seeing deep space.

All 5 cues active: foreground shapes overlap midground, shrink toward the horizon, fade into sky blue, and scroll at different parallax speeds.
