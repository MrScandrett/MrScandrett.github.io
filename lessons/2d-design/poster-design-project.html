Draw It · Lesson 3 of 3 · Grades 6–12 · 60 minutes

Poster Design Project

Brief → sketch → critique → revise

Combine vector art, color and typography into a poster for a real audience.

1. Vector Drawing Studio2. Composition & Layout Studio3. Poster Design Project

The brief: invite your community

Design a poster for a STEAM showcase. Your audience is families who may know nothing about the event. They must quickly find what, when, where and what to do next. Use a real approved event or clearly label your work as a practice event. Do not publish personal contact details.

A complete design process

  1. Plan (10 minutes). Write your audience and message. Draw three small thumbnail sketches with different focal points. Pick one and explain why.
  2. Build (20 minutes). Use this starter or your preferred editor. Choose a limited palette, one vector emblem, a clear headline and grouped details. Use the earlier vector, composition, color and typography lessons.
  3. Critique (10 minutes). Show a partner the design for five seconds. Ask them to recall the event and find its date and location. Record their actual answers below.
  4. Revise (10 minutes). Change one choice based on the feedback. Save before and after versions, and explain how the change helps your audience.
  5. Deliver (5 minutes). Export SVG for editable vector artwork or PNG for a screen preview. For printing, use Print / Save PDF and check the paper size, margins and a physical proof. These exports do not add printer bleed or a CMYK profile; ask your printer about those requirements.

Review your finished work

  • Message: a viewer can find all four required details without your explanation.
  • Craft: consistent alignment, intentional spacing, readable type and a clear silhouette.
  • Iteration: three sketches, peer feedback, and a justified before/after revision.
  • Delivery: the exported file opens correctly; original work or sources and licenses are credited.

Poster starter

Change one choice at a time. Exports include your artwork, without the teaching guides. Download your work before leaving this page.

Critique & revision notes

Finish when: Submit your brief, three sketches, before/after designs, peer feedback, credits and a final export. Explain one design decision that helped a viewer.

For younger designers: work with a partner, sketch with paper shapes, and explain your choices aloud. Extend: make a second version for a different audience and compare the results.
