Foundational STEAM lesson for Sun, Moon & Stars.

Overview

The Sun is an active yellow dwarf star (spectral type G2V) at the center of our solar system, powering Earth's climate, weather, and life through nuclear fusion. Observations from space-based solar observatories allow us to study sunspots, magnetic flux, and solar flares safely.

Watching the Sun Turn

A NASA spacecraft watched one dark sunspot for two weeks. The spot drifts across the Sun because the Sun itself is spinning.
