Falling & Gravity

Foundational STEAM lesson for Falling & Gravity.

Overview

In a vacuum with no air resistance, all objects accelerate at the exact same rate regardless of their mass. Mass cancels out because heavier objects experience proportionally more gravitational force but require proportionally more force to accelerate.

The Hammer and the Feather

Does a heavy hammer fall faster than a light feather? On Earth, air gets in the way. On the Moon there is no air, so in 1971 an astronaut tried it. Make your prediction before the video stops and asks.
