    Getting a part ready to print

    1. Open your model. STL, OBJ or 3MF, from any app. Check the size: if a 40 mm part shows as 0.04 mm, the file was saved in metres; change File units.
    2. Find the best side. Print Prep tries every big flat face down and ranks them by how much support plastic they need, how firmly they sit on the bed, and how tall they print. Or click any face to put it on the bed yourself.
    3. Think about strength. Layers pull apart most easily, so a hook or bracket printed standing up can snap along its layers. The side with the least support is not always the strongest.
    4. Download and slice. 3MF keeps the units. Turn supports on in the slicer only if Checks says the part needs them.

    Shortcuts: Ctrl/⌘ Z undo · Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z redo · Ctrl/⌘ O open · F fit view. Your file never leaves this computer.
