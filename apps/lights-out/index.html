Solve it with math, not guessing

Two facts make this puzzle easy for a computer: the order of presses doesn't matter, and pressing a light twice undoes itself. So a solution is just a set of lights to press once. The computer finds that set with algebra where 1 + 1 = 0.

About the puzzle

Lights Out was an electronic toy made by Tiger Electronics in 1995, but the idea is older: the 1978 handheld game Merlin included a similar puzzle called Magic Square.

On a 5 × 5 board, only one in four random patterns can be switched off. Every puzzle here is made by pressing random lights on a dark board, so it can always be solved, and each one has exactly four different solutions.
