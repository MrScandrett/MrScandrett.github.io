ASCII CITY

A procedural neon city rendered in real 3D — then shown to you only as letters, numbers and symbols. Every character on screen is one pixel of the 3D frame: denser glyphs for brighter light, tinted by the colour underneath.

How it works: Three.js renders the city at just 160×62 pixels (on Normal detail). Each pixel’s brightness picks a character from  .'`,:;-~=+*!?%#&$@, and its hue tints that character. Edge glyphs run a Sobel filter to find sharp changes in brightness and draw | / - \ along them. The seed in the address bar rebuilds the same city for anyone you share it with. See script.js.
