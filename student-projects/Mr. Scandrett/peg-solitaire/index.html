About the puzzle

Peg solitaire was popular at the court of King Louis XIV of France: a 1697 engraving shows a princess playing it. This cross-shaped board with 33 holes is the English version.

Every jump removes exactly one peg, so going from 32 pegs to 1 always takes exactly 31 jumps. Most games end stuck with several pegs left that can't reach each other.
