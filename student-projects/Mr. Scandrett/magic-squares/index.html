🔮 Magic Squares

Place the numbers so every row, every column, and both diagonals add up to the same total: the magic sum.

How to play

Click a square, then click a number (or type it). Each number is used once. Green totals are correct; the others show how far off they are.

Why 15 for 3 × 3? The numbers 1 to 9 add up to 45, and the three rows must share that equally.

About the puzzle

The oldest known magic square is the Lo Shu from ancient China. Legend says it appeared on the shell of a turtle climbing out of the Lo River. Every 3 × 3 magic square is the Lo Shu turned or flipped: there are only 8, and they are really all the same one.

In 1514 the German artist Albrecht Dürer put a 4 × 4 magic square in his engraving Melencolia I. Its bottom row hides the year he made it: 15 14. There are 880 different 4 × 4 magic squares.
