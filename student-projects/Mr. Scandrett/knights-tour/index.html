Warnsdorff's rule (1823)

Always jump to the square that has the fewest onward moves. Visit the hard-to-reach squares first, before they get cut off. Turn on the numbers to see the count on each square you could jump to.

About the puzzle

Knight's tours were studied in 9th-century India and Arabia. Leonhard Euler wrote a paper about them in 1759. In 1823 H. C. von Warnsdorff published the simple rule this exhibit uses. It does not look ahead at all, yet it almost always finds a complete tour.

A 4 × 4 board has no tour at all. On a 5 × 5 board there are 304 different tours that start in one corner.
