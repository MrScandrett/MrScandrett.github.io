About the puzzle

This puzzle appears in Problems to Sharpen the Young, a collection attributed to Alcuin of York, a scholar at the court of Charlemagne, from around 800 AD. Versions of it are told in Africa, Asia, and Europe.

There are exactly two shortest solutions, each with 7 crossings. The trick is that the goat has to ride back across the river once.
