      Getting around Workbench

      Build with shapes

      Click a shape to add it. Select it on the workplane or in Objects. Use its colored handles to move, turn, or scale it; Properties has sliders and exact dimensions.

      Cut a hole

      Add a second shape, choose Hole, and overlap it with a solid. Group them to keep the cut together.

      Move the camera

      Drag empty space to orbit, right-drag to pan, and scroll to zoom. On a touch screen, drag to orbit and use two fingers to pan or pinch to zoom. Use 3D, Front, Side, Top, and Fit above the workplane to reset your view.

      Select more than one

      Shift-click parts, or enable Select multiple in Objects. Then group, align, or delete the selection.
