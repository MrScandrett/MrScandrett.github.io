# Guitar composer teaching media

These two original instructional animations, poster images, caption tracks,
synthesized audio, and the matching lesson exercise tablature are dedicated to
the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

They demonstrate original classroom patterns. They are not archival footage,
recordings of either composer, or transcriptions of their compositions.

Rebuild with `python3 scripts/build-guitar-composer-media.py` (Pillow, numpy,
ffmpeg). The script uses Arial when available, otherwise DejaVu Sans.

The historical lesson portraits are separate assets hosted by Wikimedia Commons.
Their original photographer, source page, and public-domain status are credited
in each lesson. The linked modern Lágrima tablature edition retains its separate
CC BY-NC 3.0 license and is not included in this CC0 dedication.
