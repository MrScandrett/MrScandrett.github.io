Source page explicitly credits the photograph to NPS: https://www.nps.gov/meve/learn/historyculture/cliff_dwellings_home.htm . Public domain as a U.S. government work; NPS rights policy: https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm . This is a modern photograph of surviving architecture, not a reconstruction of the inhabited village. Retrieved October 28, 2026.

## American painting gallery

Four Met Open Access paintings, verified `isPublicDomain: true` in the collection API and labeled Public Domain on each museum record. Retrieved October 28, 2026. Local WebP derivatives preserve the complete composition: 1000 px gallery versions and 2200 px lightbox versions, with no upscaling. No generative imagery.

- `washington-crossing-delaware.webp` and `washington-crossing-delaware-large.webp`: Emanuel Leutze, *Washington Crossing the Delaware*, 1851. Gift of John Stewart Kennedy, 1897. [Museum source and rights](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/11417). Download: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/ad/original/DP215410.jpg
- `the-oxbow.webp` and `the-oxbow-large.webp`: Thomas Cole, *View from Mount Holyoke, Northampton, Massachusetts, after a Thunderstorm—The Oxbow*, 1836. Gift of Mrs. Russell Sage, 1908. [Museum source and rights](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/10497). Download: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/ad/original/DP-12550-001.jpg
- `landers-peak.webp` and `landers-peak-large.webp`: Albert Bierstadt, *The Rocky Mountains, Lander's Peak*, 1863. Rogers Fund, 1907. [Museum source and rights](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/10154). Download: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/ad/original/DT82.jpg
- `veteran-new-field.webp` and `veteran-new-field-large.webp`: Winslow Homer, *The Veteran in a New Field*, 1865. Bequest of Miss Adelaide Milton de Groot (1876-1967), 1967. [Museum source and rights](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/11145). Download: https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/ad/original/DP102298.jpg

## Era timeline expansion

Added October 28, 2026. Each new local image has 900 px and up-to-1800 px WebP versions (`-large`); sources smaller than these are not upscaled. Complete compositions are retained. Older gallery paintings are reused with their original 1000/2200 px versions. Each timeline image has a teaching caption, exact rights wording, and shared lightbox navigation within its era. The 1963 Trikosko photograph is labeled with the Library of Congress’s “No known restrictions on publication” advisory rather than asserting a public-domain dedication.

- `cahokia-mound.webp` / `cahokia-mound-large.webp`: Cahokia’s surviving earthworks. National Park Service · Modern photograph · Public domain, U.S. government work. [Source](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/cahokia-mounds-state-historic-site-world-heritage-site.htm).
- `landing-columbus.webp` / `landing-columbus-large.webp`: The Landing of Columbus · c. 1837. Edward Hicks · National Gallery of Art · Gift of Edgar William and Bernice Chrysler Garbisch · Public domain / Open Access. [Source](https://www.nga.gov/artworks/59906-landing-columbus).
- `francis-brinley.webp` / `francis-brinley-large.webp`: A colonial portrait · 1729. John Smibert · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Rogers Fund, 1962 · Public domain / Open Access. [Source](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/12600).
- `washington-crossing-delaware.webp` / `washington-crossing-delaware-large.webp`: Washington Crossing the Delaware · 1851. Emanuel Leutze · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Gift of John Stewart Kennedy, 1897 · Public domain / Open Access. [Source](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/11417).
- `washington.webp` / `washington-large.webp`: The first president · begun 1795. Gilbert Stuart · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Rogers Fund, 1907 · Public domain / Open Access. [Source](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/16584).
- `the-oxbow.webp` / `the-oxbow-large.webp`: The Oxbow · 1836. Thomas Cole · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Gift of Mrs. Russell Sage, 1908 · Public domain / Open Access. [Source](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/10497).
- `lincoln-antietam.webp` / `lincoln-antietam-large.webp`: Lincoln at Antietam · October 3, 1862. Alexander Gardner · Library of Congress · Public domain (1862 photograph); catalog: no known restrictions on publication. [Source](https://www.loc.gov/item/2018666256/).
- `veteran-new-field.webp` / `veteran-new-field-large.webp`: The Veteran in a New Field · 1865. Winslow Homer · The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Bequest of Miss Adelaide Milton de Groot, 1967 · Public domain / Open Access. [Source](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/11145).
- `brooklyn-bridge.webp` / `brooklyn-bridge-large.webp`: Crossing the Brooklyn Bridge · 1901. Underwood & Underwood · Library of Congress · Public domain (published 1901); catalog: no known restrictions on publication. [Source](https://www.loc.gov/item/2017648930/).
