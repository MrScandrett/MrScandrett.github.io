# Open media sources

The lesson displays local, web-optimized derivatives so the page does not depend
on third-party image hosts at runtime.

## `axolotl-wild-type.webp`

- Source: [Axolotl Wild Type.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Axolotl_Wild_Type.jpg)
- Creator: Mariblubb (Marius Maehlen)
- License: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- Changes: cropped to the head and external gills, resized, and converted to WebP.

## `tiger-salamander-adult.webp`

- Source: [An adult California tiger salamander](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:An_adult_California_tiger_salamander_(5349327007).jpg)
- Creator: Pacific Southwest Region, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service
- License: public domain as a U.S. federal government work; the source page also
  records a CC BY 2.0 release.
- Changes: cropped, resized, and converted to WebP.

## `regeneration-*.webp`

- Source: Figure 1A in Hu et al. (2022), [“Muscles are barely required for the
  patterning and cell dynamics in axolotl limb regeneration”](https://doi.org/10.3389/fgene.2022.1036641),
  *Frontiers in Genetics* 13:1036641.
- Creators: Yan Hu et al.
- License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Changes: the control-limb images at 0, 9, 21, and 47 days post-amputation were
  individually cropped from Figure 1A, placed on a dark field, resized, and
  converted to WebP. Original scale bars are retained where present.
