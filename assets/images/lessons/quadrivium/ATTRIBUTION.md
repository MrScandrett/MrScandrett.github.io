# Quadrivium image credits

The lesson uses resized WebP derivatives stored in this folder so the page remains fast and does not depend on third-party image hosting. All five images are period artwork — manuscripts and woodcuts made while the quadrivium was an active curriculum (12th–16th century) — rather than modern photographs of quadrivium-adjacent objects.

- `hortus-deliciarum-seven-liberal-arts.webp` — *Philosophia et septem artes liberales* ("Philosophy and the Seven Liberal Arts"), from the *Hortus deliciarum* of Herrad of Landsberg, composed c. 1180. The original manuscript was destroyed in the 1870 fire at the Strasbourg library; this is a faithful 1818 hand-colored copy. Public domain. Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hortus_Deliciarum,_Die_Philosophie_mit_den_sieben_freien_K%C3%BCnsten.JPG).
- `arithmetic-typus-arithmeticae-1503.webp` — *Typus Arithmeticae*, woodcut from Gregor Reisch's *Margarita Philosophica* (1503), showing personified Arithmetica judging a contest between Boethius (using written numerals) and Pythagoras (using a counting board). Public domain; digitized by the Library of Congress. Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Typus_arithmeticae_LCCN92518152.jpg).
- `geometry-typus-geometriae-1504.webp` — *Typus Geometriae*, woodcut from the same edition of Gregor Reisch's *Margarita Philosophica* (1504), showing personified Geometria with surveying and measuring tools. Public domain. Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Margarita_Philosophica_-_Typus_Geometriae.tif).
- `music-guidonian-hand-15c.webp` — The Guidonian hand, a 15th-century manuscript diagram used to teach solmization (ut–re–mi–fa–sol–la) — the pedagogical system built on Guido of Arezzo's 11th-century method for teaching musical ratio and pitch. Public domain. Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Guidonian_hand.jpg).
- `astronomy-sacrobosco-de-sphaera-13c.webp` — A page from *De Sphaera* by John of Sacrobosco, the standard university astronomy textbook of the quadrivium from the 13th century onward. This manuscript (Biblioteca Apostolica Vaticana, Pal. lat. 1400, fol. 12r) dates to the end of the 13th century. Public domain. Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_of_Sacrobosco,_textbook.jpg).
