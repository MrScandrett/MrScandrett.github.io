# Roman lesson image sources

Retrieved 2026-09-06. These are authentic published NASA photographs, engineering illustrations, and astronomical observations. No AI-generated imagery is used. Exact downloaded URLs are recorded in downloads.json. Images are served locally. NASA and its partners have not endorsed this lesson.

## Reuse basis

- [NASA media guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/) permit factual educational use of NASA content, including web pages and simulations, with source acknowledgment. NASA material is generally not subject to US copyright, but this is not a blanket public-domain claim for every partner-created image, logo, or identifiable person.
- [JPL image policy](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/jpl-image-use-policy/) governs the credited NASA/JPL-Caltech image. It is included as educational, publicly reusable mission imagery under that policy, not relabeled as exclusively federal public-domain work.
- No third-party copyright restriction was identified on the selected source records. Preserve all listed credits and source links. Do not imply endorsement or use NASA identifiers as this site's branding.

## Assets

### roman-complete.jpg
- Photograph of the assembled Roman observatory following November 25, 2025 integration at Goddard.
- Credit: **NASA/Jolearra Tshiteya**.
- [Source](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/roman-space-telescope/nasa-completes-nancy-grace-roman-space-telescope-construction/).

### roman-spacecraft.jpg, roman-optics.jpg, roman-wfi.jpg
- Engineering illustrations: right-side spacecraft view, telescope cutaway, and Wide Field Instrument assembly, respectively. These are depictions, not in-space photographs or science observations.
- Credit requested by source: **NASA's Goddard Space Flight Center**. Animator: Jonathan North (KBR Wyle Services, LLC); producer: Scott Wiessinger (KBR Wyle Services, LLC).
- [Source and full credits: NASA SVS 14181, Roman Interactive Stills](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/14181/), released August 4, 2022.
- NASA educational media reuse; contractor participation is disclosed rather than assuming every illustration is a federal employee work. Downloaded official 1024px print JPEGs, without cropping.

### nancy-roman.jpg
- Historical photograph of Nancy Grace Roman with a model of the planned Hubble telescope, 1966. The model is **not** the Roman telescope.
- Credit: **NASA**.
- [Source](https://science.nasa.gov/people/nancy-roman/).
- NASA archival photograph, generally not subject to US copyright; factual biographical educational use. Source-served 820px version, full composition retained.

### andromeda-spitzer.jpg
- **PIA26276 — The Infrared Face of the Andromeda Galaxy**. Real Spitzer Space Telescope infrared observations rendered in visible colors.
- Credit: **NASA/JPL-Caltech**.
- [Source](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/the-infrared-face-of-the-andromeda-galaxy/).
- JPL educational reuse policy above; source-served 2048px image. No alteration to the astronomical image. The activity overlays opaque tiles, revealing portions as a teaching interaction; the complete image remains available by link. Aspect ratio is preserved.
- This is **not Roman data**, does not reproduce Roman's wavelength coverage or angular footprint, and is not a new observation when the user clicks an exposure button.

## Why the activity does not use Roman science images yet

The [NASA launch release](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasas-dark-universe-seeking-nancy-grace-roman-space-telescope-launches/) anticipates Roman's first images by early 2027. No verified released Roman sky observation was located as of this retrieval. The lesson prominently labels the Spitzer substitute. A future replacement must verify the original observing telescope, release provenance, credits, and reuse terms; do not relabel a simulation or another telescope's image as Roman data.
