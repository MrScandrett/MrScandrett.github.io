The static plate contains no bridge or vehicle. Bridge members, load vectors, joints, the ambulance and its strings remain live SVG. Three adjacent SVG viewports align the photographed banks with the simulation's fixed x=170 and x=610 anchors and y=180 road. Blueprint mode replaces the plate with a plain engineering background.
