# Rube Goldberg simulator model renders

These transparent PNGs are original renders produced for the ClassroomOS
simulator from models supplied by the site owner. The raw model downloads are
not redistributed in this repository.

| `crate.png` | Crate | Quaternius | CC0 1.0 | https://poly.pizza/m/NlXe0ZJGUd |
| `baseball.png` | Baseball | Poly by Google | CC BY | https://poly.pizza/m/689iUPvWl4y |
| `bell.png` | Bell | Poly by Google | CC BY | https://poly.pizza/m/efbZhPRsTuD |

`ramp.png`, `platform.png`, `domino.png`, and `fan.png` are original ClassroomOS
models created and rendered with `scripts/render-rube-kit.py`. Their materials,
camera, lighting, and framing follow `ART-DIRECTION.md` so they share a visual
language with the credited Poly Pizza models.
