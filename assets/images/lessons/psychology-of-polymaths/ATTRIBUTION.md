# Polymaths Across Time gallery image attribution

## AI-generated conceptual panel art

The following original illustrations were generated with OpenAI's built-in image
generation tool on 2026-08-25, then center-cropped and exported as 1600 × 700 WebP
assets for `lessons/psychology-of-polymaths.html`. They contain no third-party source
imagery and intentionally exclude the historical polymath gallery.

- **`multipotentialite-mind.webp`** — Prompt: a metaphysical inner landscape in which one luminous human silhouette connects distinct worlds of music, mathematics, natural science, making, and literature; violet surreal gouache and cut-paper collage; no text or logos.
- **`research-evidence.webp`** — Prompt: three evidence streams converging through a research prism, branching athletic tracks, and a rigid maze breaking into flexible paths; teal surreal gouache and cut-paper collage; no text or logos.
- **`unfair-advantages.webp`** — Prompt: music, botany, gears, geometry, and navigation flowing through one figure into a rare glowing intersection while an old rigid lens cracks; amber surreal gouache and cut-paper collage; no text or logos.
- **`compounding-curiosity.webp`** — Prompt: a rooted anchor tree drawing orbiting streams of music, science, craft, and language into a radiant new fruit; emerald surreal gouache and cut-paper collage; no text or logos.
- **`common-doubts.webp`** — Prompt: a seeker facing shadowy curved doorways that evidence illuminates into paths toward an anchor, branching tracks, and creative fields; cobalt surreal gouache and cut-paper collage; no text or logos.
- **`practice-synthesis.webp`** — Prompt: a learner assembling puzzle-like fragments of a prism, branching paths, an anchor tree, gears, and musical rhythm into one luminous mental model; violet surreal gouache and cut-paper collage; no text or logos.

All six prompts specified a wide, age-neutral, museum-quality educational illustration
with tactile paper and ink texture, symbolic rather than clip-art imagery, and no
watermark or border.

These nine portraits were resized and cropped to a 4:5 thumbnail for the "Polymaths
Across Time" gallery in `lessons/psychology-of-polymaths.html`. Source images and
their credits are listed below.
