# Dadaism gallery image attribution

All five images were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, converted to WebP, and
resized to a 900px-wide max for `lessons/dadaism.html`. Each is public domain in
the United States; sources and specific tags are listed below.

## `hugo-ball-cabaret-voltaire-1916.webp`

- **Subject:** Hugo Ball reciting his sound poem "Karawane" in a cubist costume at the Cabaret Voltaire, Zurich, 1916.
- **Source:** [Hugo Ball Cabaret Voltaire.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hugo_Ball_Cabaret_Voltaire.jpg)
- **Credit:** Unknown photographer — taken for a publicity postcard, Zurich, 1916.
- **Rights:** Public domain — PD-US (published before 1931) and PD-anon-70-EU (anonymous work, EU copyright expired).

## `duchamp-fountain-stieglitz-1917.webp`

- **Subject:** Marcel Duchamp's readymade sculpture *Fountain* (1917), photographed at Alfred Stieglitz's "291" gallery — the only surviving photographic record of the lost original.
- **Source:** [Marcel Duchamp, 1917, Fountain, photograph by Alfred Stieglitz.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Marcel_Duchamp,_1917,_Fountain,_photograph_by_Alfred_Stieglitz.jpg)
- **Credit:** Alfred Stieglitz, published in *The Blind Man* No. 2, May 1917.
- **Rights:** Public domain — PD-1923 (published in the US before 1931).

## `dada-fair-berlin-opening-1920.webp`

- **Subject:** The opening of the First International Dada Fair (*Erste Internationale Dada-Messe*), Galerie Otto Burchard, Berlin, 5 June 1920 — Dada artists posed among their assembled works.
- **Source:** [Grand opening of the first Dada exhibition, Berlin, 5 June 1920.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grand_opening_of_the_first_Dada_exhibition,_Berlin,_5_June_1920.jpg)
- **Credit:** Unknown photographer.
- **Rights:** Public domain — PD-1923 and PD-anon-70-EU.

## `schwitters-merzbild-1a-1919.webp`

- **Subject:** *Merzbild 1A (The Psychiatrist)*, 1919 — a Kurt Schwitters "Merz" collage assembled from scavenged paper, wood, and scrap material.
- **Source:** [Merzbild 1A (The Psychiatrist) (1919).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Merzbild_1A_(The_Psychiatrist)_(1919).jpg)
- **Credit:** Kurt Schwitters (1887–1948).
- **Rights:** Public domain — PD-old-75 (author died 1948; EU copyright term expired).

## `picabia-391-n13-1920.webp`

- **Subject:** Cover of *391*, issue no. 13, July 1920 — the Dada/proto-Dada art journal edited and largely illustrated by Francis Picabia, known for its mock-technical machine drawings.
- **Source:** [Francis Picabia, 391, n. 13, July 1920.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Francis_Picabia,_391,_n._13,_July_1920.jpg)
- **Credit:** Francis Picabia (1879–1953).
- **Rights:** Public domain — PD-US-1923-abroad (published before 1931) and life+70 EU term expired (author died 1953).
