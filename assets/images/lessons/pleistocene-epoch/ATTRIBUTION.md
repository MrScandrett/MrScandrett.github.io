# Pleistocene Field Lab image attribution

All four photographs below are credited by their source pages to the National Park Service without a copyright symbol. The NPS states that images credited to NPS without a copyright symbol are public domain. See the [NPS photo usage guidance](https://www.nps.gov/kaww/learn/photosmultimedia/photogallery.htm).

The local files were resized, converted to WebP, and stripped of metadata for web delivery. Their pictured content was not altered.
