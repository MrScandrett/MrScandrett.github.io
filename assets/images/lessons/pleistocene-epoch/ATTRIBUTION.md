# Pleistocene Field Lab image attribution

The first four photographs below are credited by their source pages to the National Park Service without a copyright symbol. The NPS states that images credited to NPS without a copyright symbol are public domain. See the [NPS photo usage guidance](https://www.nps.gov/kaww/learn/photosmultimedia/photogallery.htm).

The three paintings after that are historical paleoart by Charles R. Knight and Heinrich Harder, both published before 1930 and in the public domain in the United States (and in life-plus-70 jurisdictions, since both artists died more than 70 years ago). They are used to illustrate species, not as photographic evidence—the lesson text distinguishes the two.

The local files were resized, converted to WebP, and stripped of metadata for web delivery. Their pictured content was not altered.
