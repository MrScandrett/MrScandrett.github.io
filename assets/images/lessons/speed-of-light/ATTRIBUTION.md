- **Source:** [NASA Pathways Intern Helps Validate GPS Coordinates](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NASA_Pathways_Intern_Helps_Validate_GPS_Coordinates_(AFRC2024-0181-08).jpg)
