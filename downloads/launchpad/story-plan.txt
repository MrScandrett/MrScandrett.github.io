TWO DOORS: A TWINE STORY STARTER
Original ClassroomOS starter. You may change, share, and remix it.

1. Open https://twinery.org/2/ and create a new story.
2. Double-click the first passage. Name it Start and replace its text with:

You find two doors inside an abandoned observatory. One hums. The other glows.
[[Listen at the humming door->Sound]]
[[Look through the glowing door->Light]]

3. Close the passage. Twine creates Sound and Light passages for the links.
4. Open Sound and write what happens. Add: [[Try the other door->Light]]
5. Open Light. Write a different ending. Add: [[Begin again->Start]]
6. Play the story and follow every choice. Give each ending a consequence.

MAKE IT YOURS
Who is the explorer? What are they hoping to find?
What clue helps a careful reader choose?
Can the reader return, learn something, and make a different decision?

SAVE AND RETURN
Use Publish to File to download the story HTML.
Keep it in your project folder, not only browser storage.
Import that HTML into Twine to edit again.
Open the HTML in a browser to play your finished story.
