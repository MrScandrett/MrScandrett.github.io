How to explore

In a headset: point at the floor and pull the trigger (or pinch) to teleport. Point at a planet and select it to read about it. Flick a thumbstick to turn 30°.

On a screen: drag to look around, click the floor to move there, click a planet, or use the Go buttons below. With the scene focused, the arrow keys turn and step.

Is this to scale?

No, and it can’t be. If Earth were the size of a marble, the Sun would be a beach ball 150 m away and Neptune would be 4.5 km down the road. This walk squashes the distances so all eight planets fit in a big room, and it swells the small planets so you can see them. The order is right, the size ranking is right, and the facts below are real.
