Showing: the home page

The page is drawn on the inside of a sphere around you, in screens 1280 pixels wide. Screen 1 is straight ahead. Turn to your right to keep reading, then look down for the second ring. A link that stays on this site keeps you in VR; links to other sites ask you to exit first.

Using it

  • In a headset: point and pull the trigger (or pinch) to click. Flick a thumbstick to turn 30°. The row above the first screen has Back, Redraw and Exit.
  • On a screen: drag to look around and click the page. Press Redraw if something changed and the sphere hasn’t caught up.
  • Not supported yet: typing into fields, drop-down menus, dragging, live video and 3D canvases. Exit to use those.

To open any page here from a headset, use the 🥽 VR menu → 360° page.
