Showing: the home page

The page is drawn on the inside of a sphere around you in tall 1280 × 900 panels, giving lesson widgets and cards more room before the next panel begins. Panel 1 is straight ahead. Turn to your right to keep reading, then look down for the second ring. A link that stays on this site keeps you in VR; links to other sites ask you to exit first.

The theme is the world

Whatever site theme you pick surrounds the page: Day right now. Lava Lamp fills the sphere with rising wax, Vaporwave lays a neon grid in every direction, Mandelbrot wraps a slowly revolving fractal around you, Night is a field of stars. A world that moves all around you can make some people feel unwell, so Motion is Auto: calm in a headset, still if you asked for reduced motion, full on a screen. Change it any time.

Using it

  • In a headset: point until a cyan outline confirms the control, then pull the trigger (or pinch) to click. Flick a thumbstick to turn 30°. Links redraw after opening; for buttons and settings, use Redraw only when you need an updated picture. This keeps clicks responsive.
  • On a screen: drag to look around and click the page. Press Redraw if something changed and the sphere hasn’t caught up.
  • Not supported yet: typing into fields, drop-down menus, dragging, live video and 3D canvases. Exit to use those.

To open any page here from a headset, use the 🥽 VR menu → 360° page.
