K–12 STEAM Reading Library

Professionally published fiction and nonfiction, organized by grade band and paired with the science, technology, engineering, arts, math, and systems lessons they illuminate.

Verne imagined submarines; Faraday made chemistry visible in a candle; Leonardo joined observation to design. Reading is part of doing STEAM. Grade bands below are classroom recommendations, not limits: younger readers can listen together, and older readers can compare historical science with what we know today.
Family reading guide Every reading link now opens a preview checkpoint first. Upper-grade and sensitive texts require confirmation and identify concerns such as peril, violence, strong language, or outdated historical attitudes. Grade bands guide selection; a parent or educator should preview unfamiliar unabridged texts.
