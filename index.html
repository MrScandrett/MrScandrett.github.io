Recent Updates

  1. Updated Character modeling: a real humanoid base mesh 2026-10-19

    The character creator now starts from a proper humanoid template: one smooth body with a ribcage, shoulders, glutes, knees, calves and a shaped face, in an A-pose. Pick Explorer, Scholar, Guardian, Mystic, Inventor or Athlete, then change proportions, face, hair and outfit. It exports as one solid body that is ready to print.

  2. Updated 3D Design: build anything in Workbench 2026-10-17

    Workbench is a new 3D studio in your browser. Drop in shapes (walls, roofs, columns, gears, springs, trees, gems and more), make some of them holes to cut with, group and repeat them, and animate them on a timeline. Start from a house, a fidget spinner, a phone stand or a treasure chest, then print it as STL or take it into a game as an animated GLB.

  3. Updated Deuteronomy: six lenses, hard questions, and a depth dial 2026-10-17

    Moses’ farewell speeches now unfold through six lenses (memory, the Shema, justice, limits on power, remembering, choosing life) that each link to the passage, forward into later Scripture, and to a hard question. Pick Guided, Build, or Investigate depth, test yourself with seven questions, and write a four-part reflection that saves in your browser.

  4. Updated Jim Henson: Camera Frame Lab 2026-10-17

    Be the camera operator on a puppet set: move the camera, tilt and lens until the puppet is framed and the puppeteer is hidden, then race to steer a puppet using only the monitor, which isn't a mirror. Plus an archival photo gallery.

  5. Updated Stage Plotting: from a libretto to a ground plan 2026-10-17

    Decode W. S. Gilbert's set description for The Pirates of Penzance (“Rock L. sloping down to L.C.”) into a measured ground plan with an item list you can enter in the designer. Plus photos of real stages, a fly rail, and an 1876 theatre plan.

  6. Updated Theatre Cue Sheets: a worked example from The Pirates of Penzance 2026-10-17

    See a stage manager's marked script for the opening of The Pirates of Penzance, the cue sheet it turns into, and the exact words called on headset. Plus prompt books from 1720 and 1861 and the lighting boards that answer GO.

  7. New lesson Blueprint Drafting Studio 2026-10-17

    3D Design · Plan a buildable object with aligned front, top and right views; practice scale, dimensions and line conventions on an interactive drafting board; then transfer the approved plan into any 3D CAD tool.

  8. New lesson Deuteronomy: Remember, Love & Choose Life 2026-10-17

    Bible Studies · Listen to Moses retell Israel’s story and law to a new generation preparing to cross the Jordan.

  9. New lesson Leviticus: Holiness, Worship & Neighbor-Love 2026-10-17

    Bible Studies · Discover why Leviticus places worship, justice, bodies, food, land, and neighbor-love inside one vision of holy life.

  10. New lesson Numbers: Wilderness, Trust & a New Generation 2026-10-17

    Bible Studies · Follow a rescued people through testing, failure, mercy, and preparation as a new generation reaches the edge of the land.

  11. New lesson Robot Build Studio 2026-10-17

    Engineering · Carry one classroom robot through eight connected missions: measured chassis, print planning, 3D-pen prototyping, mechanisms, servo motion, staged integration, end-effector testing, and evidence-based documentation.

  12. New lesson 3D Design: Foundation & Three Outcome Tracks 2026-10-14

    3D Design · Start with navigation, transforms and Boolean modeling, then use existing lessons to make a printable object, a finished scene, or game-ready assets. Reuse one project and prove the exported result.
