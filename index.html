Recent Updates

  1. Updated Bridge Over Troubled Water opens on the build lab 2026-11-10

    The bridge sim is now the first thing you see, with nothing covering the bridge, and full screen on a sideways phone puts the tools beside it. Triangles now have to earn their keep: after each test the lab tells you which ones actually carried load and which members did nothing. Members have a longest length, so no more one-beam shortcuts, and every phone, laptop and speed setting now gets the same result.

  2. Updated Omar Khayyam: the mathematician behind the Rubáiyát 2026-11-10

    A new pioneer lesson on Omar Khayyam: solve a cubic equation with a parabola and a circle the way he did, race his Jalali calendar against ours, bend Euclid's parallel postulate, then read the Rubáiyát in three layers (the Persian word for word, FitzGerald's 1859 and 1889 versions, with every change marked) and write your own AABA quatrain.

  3. Updated The Unit Factory is now a point-and-click factory floor 2026-11-10

    Explore the factory like an adventure game: pull the lever, flip the mode breaker, take crates off the Materials shelf and planets off the Worlds shelf, and Gauge the foreman talks you through every run. Real conversions now show their working as a factor-label chain with cancelling units and a balance that stays level. Two new bridges turn 10 kg into a to-scale cube of gold, water, cork or even air (density), and into a spring-scale reading on the Moon, Mars or Jupiter (gravity). Predict before you transform, and finish six work orders.

  4. New lesson Omar Khayyam: The Tentmaker of Science & the Rubáiyát 2026-11-10

    Language & Literature · Meet the Persian mathematician-poet: solve a cubic with a parabola and a circle, race the Jalali calendar against the Gregorian, bend Euclid's parallel postulate, read the Rubáiyát in three layers (the Persian word for word, FitzGerald 1859 and 1889), and forge your own AABA quatrain.

  5. Updated Live Sound Reinforcement: beat feedback and run the PA 2026-11-08

    A new live-sound lesson covers what happens after the mixer: decibels and the inverse square law, gain before feedback, ringing out a PA with a 31-band EQ (with sound and an ear-training mode), loudspeakers and beaming, amplifier loads and speaker cable, timing delay speakers, hunting hum and protecting your hearing, and the show-day order from load-in to debrief.

  6. Updated Path Studio is now a real vector editor: pen curves, editable points, Unite / Minus / Intersect 2026-11-08

    The Pen now pulls out curves as you drag, and every shape turns into points you can edit: drag anchors and handles, switch a point between corner and smooth, add or delete points. Select several objects at once, resize and rotate them with handles, group them, align and space them evenly, and combine overlapping shapes with Unite, Minus front, Intersect and Exclude. Rectangles get rounded corners, polygons become any number of sides or a star, and text gets real fonts.

  7. Updated The Recording Session: run a studio console, cues, patchbay and print 2026-11-08

    A new studio lesson walks through a real recording session: the channel and monitor paths, the CHOP, cue and FX sends, HearBack rules, full and half normals on the patchbay, destructive vs non-destructive solo, printing the mix, and why we record at 24-bit / 48 kHz. The EQ, Compression and Microphone lessons also gained the tone-knob-to-parametric ladder, level matching, serial vs parallel compression, stereo mic techniques and a live 3:1 rule calculator.

  8. New lesson Live Sound Reinforcement: Feedback, the PA & Show Day 2026-11-08

    Technical Elements · Run a live PA like a pro: decibels and the inverse square law, gain before feedback (PAG/NAG), ringing out a system with a 31-band EQ, loudspeakers and beaming, amplifier loads and speaker cable, delay-speaker time alignment, ground loops and hearing safety, and the show-day workflow from load-in to debrief.

  9. New lesson The Recording Session: Console, Cues & the Print 2026-11-08

    Technical Elements · Run a studio session on an in-line console: trace the channel and monitor paths, choose a CHOP source, build cue mixes and HearBack rules, patch full- and half-normalled points, compare destructive and non-destructive solo, print the mix, and see why we record at 24-bit / 48 kHz.

  10. New lesson 20Q Mind Reader & Information Theory 2026-11-05

    Computer Science · Play a retro 20Q handheld clone that guesses what you think in under 20 questions. Explore Shannon entropy, binary decision trees, information gain, and how AI optimizes decisions with bits.

  11. New lesson 3D from Scratch: Projection, Raycasting & Render Engines 2026-11-05

    Computer Science · Explore how flat 2D screens create 3D illusions: perspective division math, wireframes, DDA raycasting, Mode 7 affine floors, software rasterization with Z-buffers, and raytracing.

  12. New lesson Amelia Earhart: The Equator Flight & Radio Line 2026-11-05

    Complex Systems & Humanities · Calculate aircraft fuel reserves, plot lines of position with solar celestial sights, and analyze 1937 directional radio navigation to Howland Island.
