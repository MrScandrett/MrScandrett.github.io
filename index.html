Recent Updates

  1. Updated Fake Book note names 2026-10-13

    Show colored letter names right on the notes in both treble and bass clef, or switch to names under the staff. Meter labels now show only where the meter changes, and fewer false key labels.

  2. Updated Fake Book playback and bass staff 2026-10-13

    Click a bar, then press Play to start right there. A Click button turns the metronome and count-in off. Busy left-hand triplets now show as clean beamed groups.

  3. Updated Fake Book song map and zoom 2026-10-13

    Long songs get a song map in the play bar: click or drag to jump to any bar, zoom out, or press Fit song to see the whole piece at once.

  4. Updated Finger positions under every measure 2026-10-13

    Instead of tab, turn on Finger positions to see a small fingerboard under each measure with the fingers placed on it for guitar or violin. The hand stays put from bar to bar when it can, and a dashed ring shows where the last bar ended so you can follow the hand along.

  5. Updated Play the melody on violin 2026-10-13

    The melody panel now has a Violin tab. For every note it shows the string, which finger to use (and whether it sits low or high on the fretless board), and which position your hand is in, with a sound check from the violin voice.

  6. Updated Print any score from the Fake Book 2026-10-13

    Press Print notation to get your song on letter paper: a title block, big clear staves, and page breaks that never cut a line of music in half. Songs you imported (MusicXML or iReal) have their own print button too.

  7. New lesson Charlie Parker 2026-10-13

    Music · Explore bebop, chord tones, and melodic variation through Charlie Parker’s musical innovations.

  8. New lesson Duke Ellington 2026-10-13

    Music · Explore orchestral color, collaboration, and arranging through Duke Ellington’s music.

  9. New lesson George Gershwin 2026-10-13

    Music · Read and play melodies from three Gershwin songs, browse their original sheet music, and explore his biography and the journey from Broadway to jazz standards.

  10. New lesson Jelly Roll Morton 2026-10-13

    Music · Read and play excerpts from four Morton works, browse their original sheet music, and explore his biography, New Orleans jazz, and ensemble arranging.

  11. New lesson Learning Cuneiform 2026-10-13

    Language & Literature · Recognize Sumerian word signs, build a short sign sequence, and explore context-dependent readings.

  12. New lesson Learning Egyptian Hieroglyphs 2026-10-13

    Language & Literature · Read consonant signs, investigate reading direction, and assemble a real Egyptian word.
