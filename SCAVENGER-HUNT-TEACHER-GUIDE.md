# 📖 Scavenger Hunt Teacher's Quick-Start Guide

## 🎯 Overview
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Challenging but fair for grades 6–12
- **Team Size:** 1–2 students max
- **Reward:** Winner gets a free 3D print (queued first)
- **Goal:** Students explore ClassroomOS while learning STEAM facts

---

## ⏰ 15 MINUTES BEFORE START

### Prep Checklist:
- [ ] Print or display **SCAVENGER-HUNT-2026.md** (student questions)
- [ ] Have **SCAVENGER-HUNT-ANSWER-KEY.md** ready (teacher grading)
- [ ] Check the **Video Library count** on your site (for Q29)
- [ ] Ensure all computers/devices have internet access
- [ ] Test that ClassroomOS loads quickly
- [ ] Decide: Will students share one computer per team, or use 2?
- [ ] Set a timer (digital or physical)

### Room Setup:
- **Option A:** One computer per team (preferred)
  - Teams work independently, less chaos
  - Faster overall
  
- **Option B:** Shared computers on rotation
  - Works if device count is limited
  - Teams take turns visiting specific pages
  - May feel rushed

---

## 🚀 AT START TIME (5 MINUTES)

### Rules Briefing:
*"Today you're exploring ClassroomOS by answering 30 questions. You have 30 minutes. Work as a team of 1 or 2—no larger. You can visit any part of the website. First team to answer all 30 correctly wins a free 3D print. Let's go!"*

### Hand Out:
- Question sheet (printed or on screen)
- Answer sheet (printed or on document)

### Start Timer:
- Announce: **"You have 30 minutes. Starting... NOW."**
- Write start time on the board
- Set audible timer alarm

---

## 📍 DURING THE HUNT (25 MINUTES)

### Your Role as Proctor:

**Do:**
- ✓ Circulate and watch for fair play
- ✓ Make sure teams aren't sharing answers with each other
- ✓ Encourage struggling teams ("You're halfway there!")
- ✓ Answer process questions (e.g., "Can I use the search bar?" → Yes)
- ✓ Remind about time milestones ("5 minutes left!")

**Don't:**
- ✗ Give away answers (even hints are risky)
- ✗ Tell them which page to visit (let them explore)
- ✗ Allow teams larger than 2
- ✗ Let them continue past the 30-minute mark

### What If:
- **Team is stuck:** Say *"That's in one of the modules—keep looking!"*
- **Team asks if answer is right:** Say *"I'll tell you after time is up."*
- **Team finishes early:** Let them relax or work on a reflection sheet
- **Internet is slow:** Announce 2-minute extension if it affects everyone equally

---

## ⏹️ AT 30-MINUTE MARK

### Call Time:
- **Ring timer alarm.** Make it loud enough to hear.
- Announce: **"Pens down. Time is up. Please step away from devices."**
- Collect answer sheets immediately

### Fair Play Check:
- Verify each team's sheet is signed/identified
- Look for suspicious answer patterns (identical to another team = red flag)

---

## 🏅 GRADING & RESULTS (10 MINUTES)

### Quick Grade:
1. Open **SCAVENGER-HUNT-ANSWER-KEY.md**
2. Check each answer against the key
3. Mark correct/incorrect
4. **Total correct out of 30**
5. Record score for each team

### Announce Winner:
*"Congratulations, Team [Name(s)]! You scored [X]/30 and won the hunt. Your free 3D print is queued first. Choose your design from [list], and it will print tomorrow!"*

### Recognition:
- 2nd & 3rd place honorable mention (optional)
- "Best explorer" / "Fastest finishers" fun awards

---

## 💡 PRO TIPS

### To Make It More Engaging:
1. **Play background music** during the hunt (Lo-Fi study beats)
2. **Announce time milestones:** "15 minutes left!" to build urgency
3. **Give bonus points** for creative team names
4. **Ask the winner** which question was hardest (encourages reflection)

### To Prevent Cheating:
1. **Seat teams far apart** so they can't see each other's screens
2. **Ask them to show you the page** they found an answer on (spot-check)
3. **Watch for copy/paste** — handwritten answers are cleaner evidence
4. **Note any identical answers** between teams (red flag for sharing)

### If Finishing Early:
- Have a **reflection sheet** ready:
  - "What's one fact you learned?"
  - "Which lesson would you like to explore more?"
  - "What surprised you?"

### If Falling Behind:
- You can grant a **5-minute extension** if:
  - Internet was slow for everyone
  - There was a technical issue
  - Make it fair for all teams equally

- **Easiest questions:** Q1, Q11, Q21 are typically fast
- **Time taken by winner:** Did they finish in 15 minutes or 28?

## 🤔 FAQ FOR COMMON QUESTIONS

**Q: Can students use Google to answer questions?**
A: No—answers must come from ClassroomOS itself. This teaches them to explore the actual site.

**Q: What if two teams tie?**
A: First team to submit their sheet wins. If truly simultaneous, ask both teams one bonus question.

**Q: Can I give hints?**
A: Avoid direct hints. Try: *"It's on the STEAM Lessons page in one of the modules."* Keep it vague.

**Q: What about students who finish very quickly (< 10 min)?**
A: Possible, but unlikely if questions are accurate. Spot-check their answers—verify they visited the pages.

**Q: Should I re-do this hunt next year?**
A: Yes! Update Q29 (video count) and any lesson descriptions that change. The core structure is timeless.

## 📞 TROUBLESHOOTING

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Website is slow | Grant 5-min extension to all teams equally |
| A page won't load | Have a backup: "Try reloading" or "Move to next question" |
| A team asks for an answer | Redirect: "What page haven't you tried yet?" |
| Time management is off | Print a "mini checklist" on the answer sheet (Q1–10, Q11–20, Q21–30) |
| Tie at finish | First to turn in sheet wins; if true tie, one bonus tiebreaker Q |
| Winner absent day of | Hold their prize, announce them the next day |
